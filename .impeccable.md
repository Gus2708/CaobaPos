## Design Context

### Users
Owner and cashiers of a Venezuelan bakery-café. They work standing at the counter on a landscape tablet (phone/PWA as a backup), often during rush hour with a queue waiting. The UI is in Spanish.

Jobs to be done:
- Ring up a sale in seconds and take payment by cash, card, transfer or credit.
- Handle prices in USD and Bs using the BCV exchange rate.
- Track regulars who buy on credit and register their partial payments.
- Check and adjust inventory, prices and stock.
- Review the day's sales and profit after closing.

### Brand Personality
Warm · artisanal · welcoming.

The app should feel like the shop itself: handmade, generous, a place regulars come back to. Calm confidence at the till. Never cold, clinical or "fintech".

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: dark, warm espresso surfaces, set by the Caoba brand manual. Committed; do not flip to light.
- **Palette**: Deep Espresso `#140906`, Caoba Gold `#CD9B46`, Cream `#EEDDC0`. Sage for success, Coral for errors. Gold is reserved for the primary action and key accents; it works because it is rare.
- **Type**: Parkinsans (rounded, friendly) for all UI copy through `AppText`. JetBrains Mono only for prices, quantities and codes.
- **Motifs**: espiral, flor 1 and flor 2 appear as quiet texture (watermarks, empty states, login), never as clutter on working surfaces. Always rendered through `BrandMark`.
- **Surfaces**: 1px gold-tinted borders and soft cream reflections instead of heavy shadows. No pure white, no pure black.
- **Copy**: short, friendly Spanish. Sound like a person behind the counter, not a bank.
- **Anti-references**: cold fintech dashboards, neon or lime-on-black "tech" looks (the old Obsidian & Lime spec in `docs/design.md` is superseded), generic decorative glassmorphism.
- **Source of truth**: `lib/designTokens.ts` and `docs/DESIGN_GUIDE.md`.

### Design Principles
1. **Speed at the till beats decoration.** Every checkout interaction stays reachable, legible and tappable (44pt minimum) in the middle of a rush.
2. **Warmth through material, not ornament.** Tinted neutrals, cream reflections, rounded type and friendly copy carry the brand.
3. **Gold is a signal.** One primary action per view carries it.
4. **Money is unambiguous.** Prices and totals are always monospaced, aligned and clear about currency, in both USD and Bs.
5. **Motifs whisper.** Brand marks set the atmosphere at rest and never compete with data.
